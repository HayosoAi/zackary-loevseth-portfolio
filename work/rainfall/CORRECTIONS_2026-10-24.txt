PRECIPITATION EXPLORER — DATED TERMINOLOGY AND INTERPRETATION CORRECTION
24 October 2026

This is a reproducible NOAA precipitation explorer and data-quality/software
work sample, not a scientific discovery or independent climate validation.

PRCP denotes total precipitation, including the liquid-water equivalent of
frozen precipitation. Current rain/rainfall labels referring to PRCP were changed
to precipitation. Historical URLs and the internal Rainfall namespace remain
for compatibility. Raw observations, frozen results and historical provenance
records were preserved; prior wording remains in Git history.

Changing the dates changes the accumulated quantity. Changing a reference mean
changes the relative question and percentage by ordinary arithmetic. The selected
2–5-window means are not climate normals, and a changed label does not establish
statistical significance. The exact-sum completeness rule is a reporting policy.

The synthetic daily examples are conditional arithmetic. For complete total T,
fixed reference mean M and omitted nonnegative amount x, zero fill gives T−x.
If T<M, then T−x<M: zero sign reversals are guaranteed, not an informative test
of robustness or a meaningful failure to replicate reversal risk. If T>M, an
above-to-below reversal requires x>T−M. The published LA examples illustrate
that latter condition; they do not estimate real missingness probabilities.

Legitimate work retained: inspectable dates/reference membership, source hashes,
reproducible calculations/builds, explicit unavailable states, numerical chart
alternatives and keyboard/responsive interface work. These software checks do
not establish physical measurement accuracy or climate interpretation. Physical
station-history continuity and homogenization remain unresolved; this is one
station extract with short dependent reference contrasts.
