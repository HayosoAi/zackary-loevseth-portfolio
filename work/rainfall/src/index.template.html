04 / Synthetic demonstration

What if one month were unknown?

Every date from 1 July 2018 through 31 December 2024 is present with a PRCP value in the frozen CSV. This synthetic demonstration hides one known month at a time. It does not alter the observations or the primary comparisons above. The reference is fixed to the 2019–2023 calendar-year mean.

Strict completeness withholds the full total. Zero fill assumes the missing month’s precipitation amount was numeric zero. Scaling multiplies the observed 11-month sum by 12/11 and assumes exchangeable months despite seasonality. Neither replacement is adopted as an imputation.

05 / Evidence and boundaries

Reproducible arithmetic. Limited inference.

Observation and missingness rules

PRCP is total precipitation, including the liquid-water equivalent of frozen precipitation; it is not a rain-only measure.

2,376 unique contiguous daily dates form 78 complete calendar months. There are no missing PRCP values and no nonblank quality flags in this frozen input; all source flags are W. The 58 trace flags are retained: numeric 0.00 inches at the supplied precision does not mean physical zero precipitation.

Provider calendar-date keys are used as supplied, with leap days retained. Observation-time attributes are blank; a UTC interval or daily reporting cutoff is not inferred. Daily amounts are summed at hundredth-inch precision. Inches × 25.4 converts units without adding measurement precision.

Fixed-reference rule

For this app’s exact-sum reporting policy, a requested target or reference window must contain every calendar month. If any requested reference member is incomplete, its entire selected reference mean and percentage are unavailable. No endpoint is dropped. A zero reference denominator would make the percentage undefined.

The 24-month reference with endpoints 2019–2023 requires January–June 2018, outside this extract. Its endpoint-2019 member has 18 of 24 months; the other four complete members do not replace it. The existing 2020–2023 option uses four complete reference windows. Selecting it changes the reference set; it does not fill the months outside the extract.

The local coverage check found no date gaps or blank PRCP values within the extract. Reported zeros, including 58 trace values, are observations. A coverage cutoff cannot supply absent precipitation amounts. See the methods and completeness rule and two synthetic daily counterexamples.

Station and scope limits

Legacy NOAA HOMR remarks dated 20 May 2024 describe a move; formal records change 24 May. Neither establishes the exact observational handoff. The daily series retains USW00093134 and the old USC name while the metadata crosswalk remains unresolved. All selected targets include post-change observations.

NOAA’s station-practice biases are not homogenized. Matching numeric values and flags do not establish physical-site continuity. This point series does not represent a county or fire footprint and supports no fuel, ignition, climate-trend or causal/predictive wildfire conclusion.

Inspect and reproduce the comparison

This is a software and data-visualization demonstration. The frozen NOAA extract, calculation source and build instructions make the selected dates, reference members and completeness rules inspectable. Different references answer different relative questions; a changed label does not establish scientific significance. The offline build embeds the data, charts and controls in one page.
